How to measure properly
  1. Take off your shoes and stand straight against a wall.
  2. Heels together, back touching the wall, look straight ahead.
  3. A friend puts a flat scale on your head and marks the wall.
  4. Measure from the floor to the mark with a tape.

Measure once a month, on the same day, so you can watch yourself grow.

      Everyday foods and Sometimes foods

      There are no bad foods. Some foods are Everyday foods — dal, roti, rice, fruit, vegetables, milk. Your body uses them all day long.

      Others are Sometimes foods — jalebi, chips, cold drink, samosa. They are made for festivals, birthdays and treats. Eating one does not make you bad. Eating them every single day just leaves less room for the foods your body is waiting for.

      Drink water instead of cold drinks when you are thirsty, and always wash your hands before eating.
